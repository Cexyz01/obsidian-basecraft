# Basecraft

Advanced pivot tables for [Obsidian Bases](https://help.obsidian.md/bases).

## What it does

Adds a Pivot view to any `.base` file. Pick a row property, a column property and an aggregation, and Basecraft groups your entries into a cross-table — like a spreadsheet pivot, but native to your vault.

## Free

- Pivot view (rows × columns)
- Count and Sum aggregations
- Row, column and grand totals

## Pro

A one-time purchase unlocks:

- Average, min, max, median and distinct-count aggregations
- Drill-down: click a cell to open the matching notes
- Multi-dimensional rows and columns (nested groupings)
- Conditional formatting (heatmap)
- Percentage of total / row / column
- Export to CSV and PNG

A license activates on up to 3 devices and never expires.
